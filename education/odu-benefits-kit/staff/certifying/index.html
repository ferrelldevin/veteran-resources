Certifying officials

What reaches you from the desk, and the VA rules behind it.

How questions reach you

ODU lists Military Benefits & Certification Services, 757-683-7153 and military@odu.edu, as the contact for VA education benefits (ODU’s certifying official page). The cards below are the situations this kit’s Front-Desk Map routes to you. That routing is the kit’s suggestion for desk staff, not an ODU procedure.

Front-Desk Map lines that come to you

These cards are the situations the kit’s Front-Desk Map (revision 59, read 2026-10-15; review by 2027-08-01) routes to you. Each card opens its own line.

  • 3Registered, wants to be certifiedWhen a clearance form or a Chapter 31 authorization is due. Continuing, unchanged students are usually recertified without one
  • 4Not certified, or certified wrongA student not paid because enrollment wasn’t certified, or was certified wrong
  • 6Does this class count as in person?How the class meets, or a remedial class. VA makes in residence or distance part of your certification
  • 8A drop or a withdrawalThe last date of attendance, and any reasons in writing
  • 11A Chapter 31 authorization emailForwarded, and confirmed you have it
  • 12An authorization that doesn’t matchProgram, campus, dates or facility code, written down
  • 13Someone asked the desk to certifyThe desk says no and passes it to you
