The one rule — for everyone at the desk who is not the designated School Certifying Official (work-study students, SCO assistants, read-only users): You may assist. You never certify to VA. Certification is the designated SCO's act alone — VA: work-study students "may assist with the certification process; however… they cannot certify information to VA." Who does what — by role, never by name

AskAbout
School Certifying Official (SCO) — ODU Military Benefits & Certification ServicesCertifying your enrollment to VA; the Veterans Clearance Form; what a drop, add or change does to your certification; whether a class is certified as in residence (VA to certifying officials: "SCOs will certify all courses which combine Classroom Instruction and Distance Learning (Hybrid) as In-Residence training" — VA Education Service, Hybrid Training Updates FAQs, 2019 — for terms beginning on or after 2019-08-15; remedial and deficiency courses are the stated exception). Contact by function on odu.edu/military.
VR&E counselor (VRC) — VAEverything Chapter 31: your plan, authorizations to the school, subsistence, books and equipment, switching. Cannot reach them: VA's handbook for schools — "call or visit your VA Regional Office and ask to speak to the VR&E Supervisor. Someone will assist you in your VRC's absence." (VR&E SCO Handbook § M, on VA's KnowVA — the page renders in a browser only, a plain fetch returns its shell; read there 2026-09-02); the office itself is found at va.gov/find-locations.
Accredited Veterans Service Officer (VSO)Disability claims and ratings, appeals, other VA benefits — "always free". va.gov, 2025-01-23
VA Education Call CenterPayment status, remaining entitlement, letters, debts, for the GI Bill chapters. 888-442-4551 (TTY 711), Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–7 p.m. ET — VA's published line, quoted from the va.gov payments FAQ, updated 2026-08-05, read 2026-09-02; re-read there by 2027-08-01.
Ask VAVA: "You can use Ask VA to ask a question online. You can ask about education, disability compensation, health care and many other topics." "You should receive a reply within 7 business days." Sign in to ask about yourself. (Practical advice, not a VA rule: name the school and the term dates.) va.gov/contact-us/ask-va, read 2026-09-02
