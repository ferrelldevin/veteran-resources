# Veteran Resources

Organized, cited pages and links for veteran students and the people who help them. Every link here goes to the issuer — VA, the statute, or the school — or to a page built from those sources with the source beside each line. Nothing here is a VA decision.

The site root is the campus office's hub: every page with a printout beside it. A veteran is handed a printed sheet, a QR code, or one page's address.

## Education benefits

Which VA education benefit you are on, what a semester pays, and what the school's certifying office does with each question.

- [Veteran Benefits Kit · ODU](https://ferrelldevin.github.io/veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/) — The front door to four pages: Semester Pay Planner, Which Benefit Am I On, Front-Desk Map, and the scan sheets. Every figure quoted from VA or ODU with the source beside it; each page passed an independent judge.
- [Semester Pay Planner](https://ferrelldevin.github.io/veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/planner/) — Sessions, classes, credits → the monthly housing or subsistence figure per stretch of the term.
- [Which Benefit Am I On](https://ferrelldevin.github.io/veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/benefit/) — Find what you have; the programs side by side; who to ask.
- [Front-Desk Map](https://ferrelldevin.github.io/veteran-resources/education/odu-benefits-kit/desk/) — For the office: fourteen situations, one check, the branch, the hand-over, the source.
- [Chapter 31 training-time worksheet (case study)](https://github.com/ferrelldevin/va-chapter31-worksheet) — The kit's ancestor: turning certified credit hours into VA training time and what it pays, cited to 38 CFR — including the review that failed it. Repository.
- [Post-9/11 GI Bill rates](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/post-9-11-gi-bill-rates/) — Housing, tiers, the more-than-50% rule, exclusions. Rate year 2026-08-01 → 2027-07-31.
- [Education benefit rates — Chapters 30, 1606, 35, STEM](https://www.va.gov/education/benefit-rates/) — The other chapters' tables; the FY tables change 2026-10-01.
- [Chapter 31 subsistence rates, FY2026](https://www.benefits.va.gov/vocrehab/vrerates26.asp) — 2025-10-01 → 2026-09-30.
- [VR&E eligibility](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/eligibility/) — Apply with a 10% rating; the counselor decides entitlement.
- [How to apply for VR&E](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/how-to-apply/) — VA Form 28-1900 with a rating; 28-0588 for a service member without one, on VA's three routes.
- [Verify your school enrollment](https://www.va.gov/education/verify-school-enrollment/) — Post-9/11, Montgomery GI Bill, VET TEC 2.0 and Chapter 35 verify monthly.
- [Check remaining GI Bill and other education benefits](https://www.va.gov/education/check-remaining-benefits/) — By form: 22-1990 (GI Bill), 22-5490 (DEA), 22-1990e (transferred).
- [GI Bill and other education payment FAQs](https://www.va.gov/resources/gi-bill-and-other-va-education-benefit-payments-faqs/) — When the first payment lands; what to do if it is late; the Education Call Center.
- [GI Bill Comparison Tool — ODU-Norfolk](https://www.va.gov/education/gi-bill-comparison-tool/institution/11900146) — The housing figure VA uses for the campus; Yellow Ribbon.
- [ODU veteran student checklist](https://www.odu.edu/military-connection/veteran-student-checklist) — The clearance form; 30–45 business days for initial certification.
- [ODU military FAQs](https://www.odu.edu/military-connection/faqs) — Who files the clearance form and when; the Chapter 31 exception.
- [ODU VA education benefits — the certifying office](https://www.odu.edu/military-benefits/va-certifying-official) — The office's own page and contact.
- [ODU academic calendar](https://catalog.odu.edu/undergraduate/academiccalendar/academiccalendar.pdf) — Session dates, add/drop, withdrawal.
- [VR&E School Certifying Official Handbook (KnowVA)](https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000260798/VRE-School-Certifying-Official-Handbook) — For certifying officials. Renders in a browser only.

## Careers and employment

Getting from service to work: VA's employment programs, and tools for the paperwork.

- [resume-gate](https://github.com/ferrelldevin/resume-gate) — A resume generator whose build fails when the resume claims something the author cannot defend. Repository, with the adversarial review that shaped it.
- [Veteran Readiness and Employment (Chapter 31)](https://www.va.gov/careers-employment/vocational-rehabilitation/) — The program page: tracks, eligibility, how to apply.

## Claims, ratings and help

Where a disability claim, a rating question, or any question in writing goes — and who may charge you nothing.

- [Get help from an accredited representative or VSO](https://www.va.gov/get-help-from-accredited-representative/) — "The services an accredited VSO representative provides on your VA benefit claims are always free."
- [Ask VA](https://www.va.gov/contact-us/ask-va/) — Any question in writing; VA says to expect a reply within 7 business days.
- [Find VA locations](https://www.va.gov/find-locations/) — Regional offices, including where to ask for a VR&E Supervisor.
