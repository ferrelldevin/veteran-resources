Veteran Resources

Organized, cited pages and links for veteran students and the people who help them. Every link here goes to the issuer — VA, the statute, or the school — or to a page built from those sources with the source beside each line. Nothing here is a VA decision.

This page is the office's hub It is written for staff: everything in one place, with a printout for each page. What a veteran gets is a printed sheet, a QR code, or one page's address — not this list. The page is unlisted (search engines are told not to index it), but the address is not a password: treat it as internal, not as access control.
